Twin strikes: listening

Report 02 of the struck-shell-chaos project. A free 18-inch bronze disk, 1 mm thick, flat or with a 30 mm bow, struck at 0.80 R by a 1 ms raised-cosine force (cymsim, finite elements, float64, 5 s). Chaos is measured by how fast an infinitesimal change in the strength of the strike grows, relative to the motion, in the kinetic + bending energy of the whole plate (summed over 100 ms windows). To hear what that means, each strike also has twins B whose peak force is larger by a relative δ: B − A is silent while the two strikes agree and rises as they separate, and the stereo file (A left, B right) shimmers independently on the two sides once they have. The reference file is normalised to its own peak; the twin files share its gain.
